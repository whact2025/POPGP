## 5. New findings

Five confirmed findings, one blocking. Full evidence is in the YAML above; the essentials:

- **REG-001 (high, blocking, regression).** Unbounded mutual recursion between
  `_canonicalize_embedding` and `_canonicalize_rank_deficient_embedding`, introduced by the
  LIB-004 fix. Non-termination is **algebraic**, not a deep-stack accident: the recursive call
  receives a bit-identical matrix, because on an already-centred input `represented = U·S` and the
  thin SVD of `U·S` is `U·S·I`. RecursionError at every D on hub-at-index-0 stars and on a
  relabelled 3×3 grid; the prior candidate returns finite coordinates for the identical inputs.
  Not reachable through any supported `SubstrateConfig` today — `topology` accepts only chain and
  grid, and an exhaustive scan of every built-in hop metric produced zero hits — which is why CI is
  green and why the two adversarial verifiers split on severity.
- **REG-003 (low, regression).** The LIB-001 fix ships without memoisation: 420 full-space
  generator constructions over 28 distinct cells at the shipped chain_1d settings, 3.3×

  withdrawn.
- **MDS-005 (low, pre-existing).** The polar canonicalization is not exactly isometric near the

  Corroborated by probe (e), which shipped an unregistered acceptance gate through the entire CI

## 7. What holds up

Four rounds of de-escalation is real work, and the substance of this round is good. Being specific
about where credit is earned:

- **LIB-001 was fixed the right way.** The easy fix was to thread a family flag through
  `compute_leakage`. The builder instead *deleted* the duplicate construction and derived the
  in-cell generator from the single `build_interaction_terms` definition that `build_hamiltonian`
  already sums. That makes divergence structurally impossible for any family expressible as pair
  terms, rather than merely fixed for today's two. The decomposition identity is bit-exact (0.0) in
  every one of 16 configurations tested, including cases the committed tests do not cover.
- **STAT-002 took the hardest of three permitted branches.** Documentation-only, or widening the
  gate, were both allowed. Instead the estimator was changed to a properly weighted fit,
  agreement with the analytically independent exact Kubo-Mori coefficient improved 329×, the 5e-4
  threshold was left alone, and the accompanying test compares against a value derived from
  `kubo_mori_covariance` rather than from any fit. The conditioning was checked and is fine.
- **The wording remediation is genuinely semantic.** Round 3's guard was three literal strings.
  This round's is a sentence-level rule that catches phrasings nobody wrote down in advance — probe
  (d) confirms it, and it globs `docs/scientific_hardening/*.md`, so future documents are covered
  automatically. Its weaknesses are real and recorded, but this is a categorical improvement.
- **The CI-005 remedy survives commit**, which is the property that matters. Probe (f3) shows a
  source-level severity demotion genuinely flips `overall_pass`, and is caught four ways by the
  script *and* by a committed test that evaluates the artifact on its own terms rather than by diff
  against `HEAD`.
- **The six registered negative controls are real physics falsifiers**, verified by reading each
  one rather than trusting the registry cell. Feeding `0.4·ε + 0.7·ε²` to the quadratic gate and
  asserting `passed is False`; asserting a commuting Ising quench does *not* spread; asserting a
  cell's reduced modular Hamiltonian is blind where the KMS density is not; retaining and pinning a
  false-positive Bell-pair result rather than deleting it. That last one especially — pinning a
  known false positive as a permanent regression — is the behaviour of a project trying to be
  falsified rather than one trying to look clean.
- **RTV-004 closed a hole this chain found empirically.** The 0.7/0.3 mutation left 99 tests green
  at the prior candidate and now fails two, and the assertion is against an independently
  constructed operator rather than another call to the same routine.
- **Negative results were preserved under pressure.** A remediation round that touched 271 numbers
  in one artifact flipped no check and un-failed no example. Three of six examples still ship a
  failing headline verdict. That is the single most reassuring fact in this review.
- **The governance layer grew real teeth**, from zero. `tests/unit/test_review_guidance.py` did not
  exist at the prior candidate. Command-list divergence, the authority designation, and the typed
  independence declaration are now mechanically enforced, and the enforcement is sensitive to the
  exact original defects. GOV-006 is a critique of how far that enforcement reaches, not of whether
  it exists.

## 8. Open scientific questions

1. **The source law is still an identity plus a convention, not a derived law.** The passing
   modular-energy check is `−β·Δ⟨h_i⟩` under a *supplied* symmetric endpoint split, and the
   falsification matrix now says so. No discrete continuity current, no local conservation law, no
   covariant closure is implemented. The relabelling this round makes the claim honest; it does not
   advance it.
2. **One gate carries the falsification pressure.** Of eight swept statistics, only
   `minimum_signal_to_floor` tracks the physics monotonically and approaches its threshold (1.82×
   clear at the Ising β=0.3 corner). `significance_ratio` sits 7.4e6×–5.7e8× above its threshold,
   is non-monotone in β and is roundoff-dominated. A sweep whose acceptance rests on one binding
   statistic is thinner evidence than eight passing checks suggest.
3. **Emergent dimension remains parameter-dependent.** `D*` and MDS stress are not unique through
   an exactly degenerate eigenspace; the fix makes the choice reproducible, not canonical. At
   λ_dim = 0.55 the 5-cycle can select `D*=1`, and the resulting embedding is correctly gated to
   `poor_fit`. The claims matrix already labels this "benchmark-supported, parameter-dependent".
4. **Cross-platform reproducibility is attested, not demonstrated, to this reviewer.** Structured
   artifacts are compared semantically with declared tolerances; image bytes are not hashed across
   environments; and the historical Linux-only failure at `adbfab58` still has no identified root
   cause and no dedicated unit test.
5. **The KMS reference gate does not scale.** The dense nuclear-norm check is appropriate only for
   the small exact backend and deliberately excludes alternate KMS representations. Nothing in the
   scalable path enforces the reference-state condition.
6. **One unverified observation, recorded but not carried as a finding.** One reviewing pass
   reported that `docs/framework.tex:511` and `docs/framework.md:887` disagree on the Emergent
   locality row (status label, QCMI implementation gap, and the failing non-geometric control).
   That observation received no adversarial verification pass, so it is not asserted here as a
   finding; it is worth checking when the manuscript is next touched, since
   `REPRODUCIBILITY.md:20` designates the `.tex` rendering as authoritative.
